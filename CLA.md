Version 1.0 — 2026-08-11

contributions are accepted. It exists so that Pigeonpost can stay open to contribution while a single
steward — Bekir Dağ ("the Maintainer") — remains able to license and commercialize the project,
including operating the paid handle registry.

By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch, or any other work) to a Pigeonpost repository,
you agree to the following. If you are contributing on behalf of an employer or other entity, you
confirm you are authorized to bind that entity, and "you" means that entity.

> Contributor License Agreement and adds a commercial-licensing grant. It must be reviewed by counsel
> before it is relied on, and the review is tracked in the planning notes, not here.

## How to accept

Contributions are gated on acceptance of this CLA. Until an automated CLA check is in place, accept
by adding a line to your first pull request:

```
I have read the CLA and I accept it: <your name>, <the email on your commits>
```

For an entity, name the entity and confirm your authority to bind it.
