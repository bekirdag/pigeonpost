# Example Co HR policy handbook (synthetic)
This document contains synthetic test data for demo purposes and does not constitute professional advice.

## Annual leave
Employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Requests must be submitted at least 14 days before the intended start date. Unused leave cannot be carried over beyond February 28 of the following year. A maximum of five consecutive days off is permitted without manager approval.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 fully paid sick days per year for minor illnesses. A medical certificate is required if absence exceeds three consecutive calendar days. Salary continuation occurs at 100 percent for the first ten days and 80 percent thereafter. All sick leave must be reported to the supervisor by 9:30 AM on the first day of absence.

## Remote work
Staff may work remotely up to two days per week with prior approval. A home office allowance of 50 EUR is provided monthly for eligible remote employees. Internet connectivity requirements mandate a minimum download speed of 100 Mbps. Remote workers must remain available during core hours from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

## Expenses
Business travel expenses are reimbursed up to a cap of 200 EUR per day for accommodation. Meals on business trips are covered at a flat rate of 35 EUR per day. Receipts must be uploaded to the internal system within seven days of expenditure. Personal entertainment costs are not eligible for reimbursement under any circumstances.

## Equipment
Each new hire receives a laptop with a replacement cycle of every four years. Mobile phone plans are subsidized up to 40 EUR per month for job-related communication. Employees are responsible for minor damages exceeding 50 EUR in value. IT support tickets must be logged within 24 hours of equipment failure.

## Code of conduct
Employees must maintain professional behavior and respect colleagues at all times. Harassment or discrimination results in immediate suspension pending investigation. Confidential information must not be shared outside the company without written consent. Violations of this policy may lead to termination after a documented warning process.
