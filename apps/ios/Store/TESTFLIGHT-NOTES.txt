Get a handle is now directly in Settings. The purchase page shows the next App Store subscription by name, its local yearly price, and a clear retry action if subscriptions cannot load.

To test: sign in, open Settings > Get a handle, enter an available name, tap Check availability, then Buy for the displayed yearly price. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox. After registration, Settings > Handles lists the name and opens its inbox. Return to Get a handle to register another name, up to ten independent subscriptions. Restore purchases recovers existing purchases; an unfinished registration does not require another payment.

Existing handles remain on Settings > Handles. The mailbox ordering, latest-message scrolling, and copy-address improvements from the previous build are included.
