Pigeonpost 1.1 (50) for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Live streams in groups: when someone is live, the group shows a red LIVE banner; tap Watch to open the stream full screen. Admins (or everyone, if the group allows it) can Go live with the camera; the streamer can invite a viewer onto the stage, and nothing is opened on the viewer's device until they accept. Streams are end-to-end encrypted and are not recorded. On iPhone and iPad, streaming and speaking on stage show as a call in progress.

Meeting notes: open Meeting notes from the top of the inbox list (or the sidebar on iPad and Mac) to read the summaries and transcripts of calls where you agreed to notes. They are decrypted on this device with its own notes key. During a call, the host can ask for notes; everyone sees the disclosure and must agree before any audio is captured, and only your own microphone is ever captured.

The message box keeps the keyboard after Send, so the next message can be typed at once.

Please test on a physical device: watching a stream with the ring switch on silent, going live and ending the stream from the lock screen call controls, accepting a stage invitation, and opening meeting notes from a call taken in this app.
