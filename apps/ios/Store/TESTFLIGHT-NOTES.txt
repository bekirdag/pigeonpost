Conversations open at the newest message and initially display the latest ten messages. Scroll up to read older messages in batches, or use the down-arrow button to return to the latest. Incoming mail leaves your reading position alone.

The mailbox picker keeps your main address first, followed by other handle roots, named subaddresses, and key addresses. The selected mailbox has a checkmark. The duplicate address bar below inbox search is removed; copying remains available in the picker and Settings.

Get a handle focuses on registering a new name. Existing handles and renewal controls are in Settings > Handles. Up to ten yearly handle subscriptions, restoring purchases, and account ownership across devices remain supported.
