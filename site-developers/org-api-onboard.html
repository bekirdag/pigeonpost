Onboard a new employee

When your HR system hires someone, it can give them a Pigeonpost address in your org, put them in the right rooms and send them a welcome, all through the Enterprise API. Offboarding is the reverse.

You need a key with members:write and rooms:write (and messages:send with a send_as agent, for the welcome). The person who creates the key must be allowed to manage people.

1. Invite them#

The answer is 201 with the new member (status invited) and the invitation:

  • delivery: queued means Pigeonpost sends the invitation to the contact address. With copy_link, deliver the link yourself, for example in the welcome pack.
  • The link is single-use and returned only in this response (Cache-Control: no-store). Do not log it.
  • The person accepts by signing in with the invited contact address; if your org requires MFA, they set it up then.
  • Use your HR system's employee id in the Idempotency-Key: a retry after a timeout returns the same invitation instead of a 409 already_invited.

The API invites ordinary members only. Roles are granted by a person in the portal.

Common refusals: 409 seat_limit (buy seats first), 409 handle_taken, 409 domain_not_verified (your org only invites verified domains) and 400 invalid_handle.

2. Add them to rooms#

Rooms with auto_join pick up new members automatically when they accept. For other rooms, send a room invitation:

Rooms are end-to-end encrypted. The invitation appears in the person's app; when they accept, a room member's device adds them to the encryption group. The API never adds anyone to a room's keys.

Offboarding#

Suspend first if you need to stop access right away, then remove:

Removal revokes memberships, rooms, delegations and any device credentials the person approved. With mode=reserve (the default) the address /acme/jane is held for a successor: invite the next person to the same handle. The old mailbox keeps its history, detached from the org, and is not handed to anyone. mode=delete releases the address instead.

The owner and members with an admin role are out of a key's reach (403 forbidden); a person handles those in the portal.
