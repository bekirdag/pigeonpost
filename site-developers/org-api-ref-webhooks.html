POST /webhooks#

Create a webhook

Scope: webhooks:write

Registers an HTTPS endpoint for the given events (or ["*"]). The signing secret is returned once (no-store). At most 10 per org. The URL's host is resolved now and at every delivery; private, loopback, link-local and other non-public addresses are refused.

Parameters

NameInTypeRequiredDescription
Idempotency-Keyheaderstring1-255 printable ASCII characters. Scoped to the key and route; a replay within 24 h returns the recorded response with Idempotent-Replayed: true. Reusing it for a different request is 409 idempotency_conflict.

Request body

application/json

{
  "url": "https://siem.example.com/hooks/pigeonpost",
  "events": [
    "member.joined",
    "member.removed"
  ],
  "description": "SIEM"
}

Responses

201 Created.

{

      "id": "wh_l2v6a376dfbkghh3usy7oy7hm5",
      "url": "https://siem.example.com/hooks/pigeonpost",
      "events": [
        "member.joined",
        "member.removed"
      ],
      "description": "SIEM",
      "status": "active",
      "created_at": "2026-10-27T10:15:00Z",
      "created_by": "okey_eglu7kwqtraqqt7h5a3mbd7dtu",

POST your endpoint#

Event delivery (to your endpoint)

Pigeonpost POSTs each event to every webhook subscribed to it. Verify Pigeonpost-Signature before trusting the body: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>, where each v1 is HMAC-SHA256 with the webhook secret over the exact bytes t + "." + raw body. During the 24 hours after a rotation there are two v1= entries (new secret first); accept either. Compare in constant time, reject timestamps more than 5 minutes from your clock, and deduplicate on Pigeonpost-Event-Id: delivery is at least once.

A 2xx within 10 seconds is success. Anything else, including any 3xx (redirects are never followed), is retried after 30 s, 2 min, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h, then every 2 h until 24 h after the event, then marked failed.

Events: member.invited, member.joined, member.suspended, member.removed (member_id, handle); agent.created (agent, handle); agent.connected, agent.disconnected (agent, device_id); room.created, room.deleted (room_id); billing.status_changed (status, previous_status). meeting.brief_ready and security.ssh_session are reserved and not emitted yet.
