Questions
Can I cancel?
Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew.
Do I get an invoice?
Yes. Every payment comes with an invoice you can download from Billing.
Is VAT included?
The price shown is the amount charged. Turkish invoices carry VAT as required.
Where is the data?
On servers Pigeonpost runs itself; the privacy policy says where and for how long. Rooms are end-to-end encrypted.
Can admins read our messages?
No. Admins see addresses, status and last activity, never message content.
