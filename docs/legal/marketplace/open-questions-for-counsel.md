# Nests marketplace: open questions for counsel

These are the questions the drafts in this folder cannot answer. Each needs a lawyer (L) or an
accountant (A). The settled facts are in [publisher-agreement.md](publisher-agreement.md) section 1
and in `docs/billing-tax.md`.

## Structure

1. **Seller model (L, A).** Piyote is the seller of record. Does Piyote buy each app from the
   publisher and resell it (so the publisher invoices Piyote and Piyote's "commission" is a
   margin), or does it sell as the publisher's agent (komisyoncu) and invoice a commission? The
   answer drives the invoices, VAT, withholding and the wording of both agreements.
2. **Which company contracts (L).** Piyote sells; Wodo builds, reviews and hosts. Which company
   signs the Publisher Agreement, and which is the processor towards buyers? Is an
   intra-group agreement between Piyote and Wodo needed?
3. **Stored value and payment services (L).** There is no wallet and no prepaid credit, but Piyote
   collects money and holds the publisher's share and a reserve for [hold days] before paying out.
   Is this a payment service or electronic money activity under Law No. 6493, or does the seller
   model above keep it outside the law?
4. **Marketplace status (L).** Is Piyote an intermediary service provider or an electronic commerce
   marketplace (aracı hizmet sağlayıcı / elektronik ticaret pazar yeri) under Law No. 6563 as
   amended in 2022, and what duties follow (licence thresholds, ETBİS registration, information
   duties, takedown)?

## Tax and invoices

5. **e-Invoice and e-Archive (A).** Which documents does each party issue? Piyote to the buyer
   (e-Arşiv or e-Fatura); the publisher to Piyote (company publishers: e-Fatura; individuals:
   gider pusulası or self-billing?); any commission invoice from Piyote to the publisher.
6. **Withholding on individuals (A).** Does Piyote have to withhold income tax (stopaj) on payouts to
   individual publishers, at what rate and on what base, and can an individual use an exemption
   (for example for software as a copyright work)? What must they give us before the first payout?
7. **VAT on sales (A).** KDV on sales to buyers in Türkiye; treatment of sales to buyers outside
   Türkiye (export of services exemption?); reverse-charge wording for EU and UK businesses, as in
   `docs/billing-tax.md`.
8. **Digital services tax (A).** Could Piyote's marketplace income fall under the digital services
   tax (Dijital Hizmet Vergisi, Law No. 7194)?

## Consumers

9. **Withdrawal for digital content (L).** Does the exception in Article 15 of the Distance
   Contracts Regulation (services performed at once electronically; intangible goods delivered at
   once) cover one-off Nest apps and monthly subscriptions? How must the consumer's prior consent
   be collected and recorded? What must the pre-contract information and the distance sales
   contract contain?
10. **72-hour deletion (L).** Is deleting a monthly app's data 72 hours after a failed payment fair
    and enforceable against consumers, with the reminders described? Is any longer notice required?
11. **Auto-renewal (L).** What notice and cancellation rules apply to monthly card renewals for
    consumers in Türkiye?

## Data protection

12. **Roles (L).** Is the buyer the controller, Pigeonpost the processor and the publisher the
    sub-processor under KVKK and the GDPR? How do roles work for an individual buyer using an app for
    personal purposes?
13. **Contracts and VERBIS (L).** Is a separate data processing agreement needed with each publisher
    and each business buyer? Do Piyote and Wodo need to update their VERBIS registrations?
14. **Transfers (L).** If an app's declared destination is outside Türkiye, what basis under KVKK
    Article 9 (as amended in 2024) is needed, and who files any required notice?

## Later

15. **Publishers outside Türkiye (L, A).** What changes to pay publishers abroad (withholding under
    tax treaties, foreign-currency payouts, invoices, sanctions checks)?
16. **Governing law and courts (L).** Which law and courts for publishers and for business buyers?
    What must stay for consumers (Tüketici Hakem Heyeti, consumer courts)?
