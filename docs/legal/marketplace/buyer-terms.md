# Nests Marketplace Buyer Terms

Version: [version] · Effective date: [effective date]

These terms apply when you buy or use a Nest app from the Nests marketplace. They add to the
Pigeonpost terms of service and the [privacy policy](../../../site/privacy.html).

## 1. What a Nest app is

- A Nest is an AI agent that Pigeonpost runs for you in its own isolated sandbox on our servers.
- A Nest app is software made by another company or person (the **publisher**). You install it into
  one of your Nests to give that Nest new abilities.
- Each Nest app runs inside your Nest's sandbox. It cannot reach other Nests or other customers'
  data.

## 2. Who sells it

- On the web, the seller is **Piyote Bilişim ve Danışmanlık Hizmetleri Ticaret Ltd. Şti.**,
  Türkiye ("Piyote"). [Registered address, MERSIS number, tax office and number, contact address.]
- Wodo Teknoloji A.Ş. operates the Pigeonpost service and runs the apps.
- The publisher makes the app. The publisher's name is shown on the listing.
- Your contract to buy the app is with Piyote. You pay Piyote, and Piyote issues your invoice.
- Nest apps are bought only on the web. The Pigeonpost apps for phones and computers only let you
  use apps you already own. They do not sell Nest apps.
- EU and UK consumers cannot buy Nest apps on the web yet. EU and UK businesses with a verified VAT
  number can buy without VAT under the reverse charge.

## 3. How review works, and its limits

- The publisher gives us the source code. We build every release ourselves, from that source.
- We scan it, run an AI review on models that we run ourselves, test its behaviour in a sandbox,
  and a member of our staff approves it. The AI review is advisory only. A person decides.
- Only releases we approved can be bought or installed.
- Review lowers risk. It does not remove it. We cannot promise that an app is free of errors, fits
  your purpose, or will always work.
- If we find a problem later, we may quarantine or revoke a release. See section 8.

## 4. What an app can do in your Nest

- An app can read and use the data in the Nest it is installed in, as its listing describes.
- An app reaches the outside network only through the Pigeonpost broker, and only to the
  destinations shown on its listing.
- An app cannot act outside Pigeonpost (for example post, pay, change or send something elsewhere)
  unless you approve that exact action first.
- Read the listing before you install. It says what the app does, what data it uses, and where it
  connects.

## 5. Terms: one-off and monthly

There are two kinds of purchase. The listing shows which one applies and its price, with taxes.

- **One-off.** You pay once. You may use the release you bought, and updates the publisher offers
  under the same listing, [for as long as the app is offered / for [period]].
- **Monthly.** You pay each month by card through iyzico. The subscription is for one app in
  [one Nest / the Nests listed on your order].

There is no wallet, balance or prepaid credit. Each purchase is paid at the time.

## 6. Renewal and cancellation

- A monthly app renews automatically each month on the same day, at the price shown when you
  bought it, until you cancel.
- We tell you before a price change. A new price applies only from the renewal after [notice
  period], and you may cancel before then.
- You can cancel at any time in [where: account → Nests → app]. Cancelling stops the next renewal.
  The app keeps working until the end of the month you already paid for.
- Removing the app from your Nest does not cancel the subscription by itself. [Owner: decide
  whether uninstalling should cancel.]

## 7. If a monthly payment fails

- If we cannot take a renewal payment, the app is **paused at once** at the due time. The rest of
  your Nest keeps working.
- We send you reminders.
- If you pay within 72 hours, the app resumes.
- If you have not paid **72 hours** after the due time, **the app's data in that installation is
  deleted**. This cannot be undone. Export anything you need before then.
- This rule applies only to marketplace apps. It never touches your Inboxes, handles,
  organisations or first-party Nest subscriptions. Those have their own terms.
- Deleted app data may remain in encrypted backups until they expire, as described in the privacy
  policy. It is not restored from them.

## 8. Quarantine and revocation

- We may stop sales of an app, pause it in your Nest, or remove a release if it breaks our rules or
  creates a security, privacy or legal risk.
- We act at once when the risk is urgent, and tell you what happened.
- What you get back in that case is set out in the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).

## 11. Your data and privacy

- You decide what data you put in your Nest and what the app does with it. You are the data
  controller for that data.
- Pigeonpost processes it for you as processor. The publisher processes it through the app as our

- Nests are not end-to-end encrypted. To run, the app and our servers read the data it works on.
- We give publishers only redacted operation records, such as counts, status and errors. We do not
  give them your messages, files, names, handles or contact addresses.
- When you uninstall an app, its data in that installation is deleted [at once / after [period]].
- Details are in the [privacy policy](../../../site/privacy.html), in the Nests and Nest apps
