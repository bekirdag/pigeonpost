# Nests Marketplace Refund Policy

Version: [version] · Effective date: [effective date]

This policy covers Nest apps bought on the web from the Nests marketplace. The seller is Piyote

## 1. Your right of withdrawal in Türkiye

- If you are a consumer, Law No. 6502 on Consumer Protection and the Distance Contracts Regulation
  (Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği) give you, as a rule, a right to withdraw from a distance
  contract within 14 days without giving a reason.
- The Regulation excludes contracts for services performed at once in electronic form and for
  intangible goods delivered to the consumer at once (Article 15). A Nest app is installed and
  starts working as soon as you buy it.
- So, before you pay, we will ask you to confirm that you want the app to start at once and that
  you understand you then lose the right of withdrawal. We will confirm this in your order record.
- If you do not confirm, [the purchase cannot be completed / the app starts only after the
  withdrawal period]. [Owner and counsel: decide.]
- **[Counsel: confirm that the Article 15 exception applies to one-off Nest apps and to monthly
  subscriptions, how consent must be collected and recorded, and what the pre-contract
  information (ön bilgilendirme) and distance sales contract (mesafeli satış sözleşmesi) must
  contain.]**
- Your other rights under the law, for example for a defective service, are not affected.

## 2. One-off purchases

You get a full refund if:

- the app does not install or does not start, and neither we nor the publisher can fix it within
  [fix period]; or
- the app is materially different from its listing; or
- you were charged twice or by mistake.

Otherwise, a one-off purchase is not refunded once the app has started, except as section 1 or the
law requires.

Ask within [request window] of the purchase.

## 3. Monthly subscriptions

- You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal. The app keeps working until the
  end of the month you paid for.
- We do not refund the rest of a month you cancel in, except in the cases in section 2, 4 or 5.
- If you were charged for a renewal after you cancelled, we refund that renewal in full.
- If a renewal fails, the app is paused at the due time and its data in that installation is
  deleted 72 hours later if you do not pay. You are not charged for the time it was paused. See
  the [Buyer Terms](buyer-terms.md).

## 4. Problems caused by the publisher

- If the app stops working for a reason on the publisher's side and is not fixed within [fix
  period], tell us.
- For a monthly app, we refund [the affected days / the affected month].
- For a one-off app, we refund [in full / in proportion to the time it worked], depending on how
  long it worked before the failure.
- Contact Piyote, not only the publisher. Piyote is the seller and handles the refund.

## 5. If Pigeonpost quarantines or revokes an app

- If we pause or remove an app for safety, legal or policy reasons, and it is not restored within
  [restore period]:
  - for a monthly app, we stop renewals and refund [the unused part of the current month];
  - for a one-off app, we refund [in full if within [period] of purchase, otherwise in proportion].
- If we removed the app because of how you used it (for example misuse or breaking the Buyer
  Terms), no refund is due.

## 6. Chargebacks

- Please contact us before you ask your bank for a chargeback. Most problems are faster to solve
  directly.
- If you open a chargeback, we may pause the app concerned while the bank decides.
- If the bank decides in your favour, we do not charge you again. If it decides against you, the
  original charge stands.
- Opening a chargeback does not by itself close your account or affect your other Pigeonpost
  services.

## 7. How to ask for a refund

- Send a message to [support contact address] with your order number, the app, and the reason.
- We answer within [response time].
- Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with, through iyzico. Your bank may take
  [bank time] to show it.
- Refunds are made in the currency you paid in. We do not refund in cash or as a balance.
- If you received an invoice, we issue the matching correction document.
