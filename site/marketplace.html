Pigeonpost Marketplace

Services and apps from approved publishers

Every listing is reviewed and approved by Pigeonpost staff before it appears here. You buy and run them from your Pigeonpost account.

How the Marketplace works

Every release is reviewed

Pigeonpost builds every app release from the publisher's source code and never runs an image a publisher uploads. Each release is scanned and behaviour-tested, and named Pigeonpost staff approve it before it can be listed. A new version goes through review again.

Each buyer runs their own copy

When you install an app into a Nest, you get your own isolated worker, with its own sandbox and storage. It cannot reach the internet, other Pigeonpost services or other buyers' Nests.

Buying

Open a listing in your account at account.pigeonpost.dev, under Products → Services. The price is shown before you pay. Purchases are sold by Piyote Bilişim on behalf of the publisher. A monthly app can be cancelled any time and keeps working until the end of the paid month.
