Marketplace

Apps from approved publishers, installed into your Nest

The Marketplace offers apps from approved publishers that you install into a Nest. The catalog is just opening, and listings are being added.

Every release is reviewed

Pigeonpost builds every release from the publisher's source code. We never run an image a publisher uploads. Each release is scanned and behaviour-tested, and named Pigeonpost staff approve it before it can be listed. A new version goes through review again.

Each buyer runs their own copy

When you install an app, you get your own isolated worker, with its own sandbox and storage. It cannot reach the internet, other Pigeonpost services or other buyers' Nests.

Buying

Browse the catalog in your account under Products → Services. An app is a one-off purchase or a monthly subscription, bought on the web at pigeonpost.dev/account. The price is shown before you pay and includes tax. Purchases are sold by Piyote Bilişim on behalf of the publisher and paid through iyzico. A monthly app can be cancelled any time and keeps working until the end of the paid month. The Pigeonpost apps show what you own.
