Pigeonpost app privacy

Last updated 12 September 2026

This policy covers the hosted Pigeonpost app and its sign-in and messaging services at auth.pigeonpost.dev and postbox.pigeonpost.dev. The operator is Wodo Teknoloji A.Ş. (Wodo Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi), Türkiye. Contact us at bekir.dag@wodonetwork.com.

Information used by the app

  • Account information: your account identifier, email address, display name and sign-in provider information. We use these to sign you in, link your devices, secure your account and determine eligibility for tester features.
  • Messages and contacts: messages you send and receive, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, timestamps, read status, contact names, aliases, blocking choices and agent permissions. These make conversations and access controls work across your devices.
  • Files you choose: photos, documents, audio, videos or other attachments you select to send. The app does not upload your photo library or read your device address book automatically. Camera access is optional and is used to scan a sign-in QR code.
  • Handle purchases: subscription identifiers, purchase tokens or transaction identifiers, an account identifier, renewal status, expiry and the handles associated with a purchase. Google Play or Apple processes payment. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Service and security records: network addresses, request times, errors and abuse-prevention records processed by our hosting and sign-in services to operate and protect the service.

Hosted messaging and security

Connections to the hosted service use HTTPS. The hosted postbox manages mailbox keys and processes message content and attachments to send, receive and display them. This hosted app is not a service where only your device holds the decryption keys. The separate Pigeonpost protocol and independently operated nodes can have different security and retention arrangements.

Sign-in credentials stored locally by the Android app are protected using Android Keystore encryption. Signing out clears the app's session and local drafts. Protect your device and only grant agent permissions to people and agents you trust.

Why information is processed and who receives it

We use this information to provide your account, deliver messages and files to recipients you choose, synchronize your account, register handles, verify subscriptions, provide support and prevent abuse. Our hosting, sign-in and payment providers process information needed for these services. Data may be processed outside your country, including where our hosting and payment providers operate.

Your chosen recipients receive the messages, files and sender details you send them. Connected agents can access the mailboxes and information you authorize. Handles are addresses intended to be shared and resolved by other users. We do not sell personal information or use advertising or cross-app tracking in the app.

Retention and your choices

Account information, contacts, messages and attachments are retained to provide your account until you remove them or request account deletion. Deleting a message or thread removes your copy; it does not recall a copy already delivered to another user. Billing and limited security records may need to be retained for payment reconciliation, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Where retention is required after a deletion request, we will explain the relevant information and reason in our response.

You can change contacts and permissions, remove messages and threads, sign out, manage subscriptions in the store, and ask us to access, correct, export or delete your account information. Optional camera and file permissions are controlled by Android. Subscription cancellation stops future renewals; it is separate from deleting your Pigeonpost account.

Delete your account

Request deletion of your Pigeonpost account and associated data. This page works without installing the app. The same link appears under Settings → Delete account. You can also contact bekir.dag@wodonetwork.com from the email used to sign in.

Children and changes

The hosted app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We update this page when the app's data practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
