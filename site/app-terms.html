The paid storage allowance starts after Apple verifies your purchase, even before you register a name. Storage is shared by messages and attachments within each mailbox.

The App Store offers one yearly plan at a time, with capacity for one to ten custom names in a single subscription group. The U.S. base price ranges from US$8 per year for one name to US$80 per year for ten names. Upgrades replace the current plan immediately; downgrades take effect at renewal. After a downgrade, the earliest registered names remain active up to the new plan limit; other names enter a 30-day recovery period. Google Play continues to offer up to ten separately billed annual handle subscriptions, each providing one name while active, at a U.S. base price of US$8 per name per year. The store displays the actual local price, applicable taxes and renewal terms before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play payment method at confirmation.

Organizations

A company can buy Pigeonpost for its team at enterprise.pigeonpost.dev. The person who buys it is the organization's owner and accepts these terms for the company. The company owns its address space (for example /acme), its agent addresses and its rooms, and chooses who is invited and what role they have. Company plans renew automatically every month or year until cancelled; the price, seats and renewal terms are shown before payment. Company plans are sold by Piyote Bilişim and the payment is processed by iyzico. If a payment fails, the organization stays usable for 7 days, then becomes read-only for 14 days, then is suspended; its data is kept for 30 more days before deletion.

If you join an organization, your company mailbox sits in your own account but the company controls it: its admins can suspend or remove it and give the address to someone new. They see your company address, name, role, status and last activity, never your message content, and a new holder of your address does not receive your history. You can leave by asking an admin, and your personal account and personal mailboxes are not affected. Admins must not use the organization to invite people who have not agreed to receive an invitation, and are responsible for the agents and rules they set up.
