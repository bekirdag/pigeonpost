Pigeonpost Cloud and annual subscriptions

App Store subscriptions provide ongoing hosted messaging and attachment storage, available across the Pigeonpost apps, plus delivery through custom names. Free accounts include 20 MiB of storage per mailbox. Every active Pigeonpost Cloud plan includes 1 GiB per mailbox on your account; higher plans include more custom names, not additional storage. The paid storage allowance starts after Apple verifies your purchase, even before you register a name. Storage is shared by messages and attachments within each mailbox.

The App Store offers one yearly plan at a time, with capacity for one to ten custom names in a single subscription group. The U.S. base price ranges from US$8 per year for one name to US$80 per year for ten names. Upgrades replace the current plan immediately; downgrades take effect at renewal. After a downgrade, the earliest registered names remain active up to the new plan limit; other names enter a 30-day recovery period. Google Play continues to offer up to ten separately billed annual handle subscriptions, each providing one name while active, at a U.S. base price of US$8 per name per year. The store displays the actual local price, applicable taxes and renewal terms before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play payment method at confirmation.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled through the store where you purchased them. For Apple subscriptions, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends; your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation normally stops future renewal while access continues until the paid period ends. Refunds, failed payments, holds and expiry can end handle access according to the store's subscription status. Refund requests are handled under the applicable store policy and any rights required by law.

Choose an available, permitted name. A name is registered only after the payment has been verified and the name has been assigned. If a selected name is unavailable after payment, the active paid slot remains available in your account so you can choose another name without another payment. Use Restore purchases after reinstalling or if confirmation is interrupted. A purchase remains linked to the Pigeonpost account used at checkout.

When your last qualifying paid service expires or is revoked, your storage allowance returns to the free tier.
