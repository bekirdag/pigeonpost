Pigeonpost app support

Pigeonpost Inbox is provided by Wodo Teknoloji A.Ş. For help with the iOS or Android app, contact bekir.dag@wodonetwork.com. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Never send your password, connector key, or payment card details.

Sign in and receive messages

Use Sign in in the app. Your existing Pigeonpost account works across devices. A paid handle is optional: your cryptographic address can already send and receive messages.

Handles and purchases

Open Settings to register a handle. Each paid handle has a separate annual subscription; you can hold up to ten. The purchase sheet shows your local price and renewal terms. Use Restore purchases after reinstalling, or to finish assigning an already purchased handle without paying again.

Manage or cancel renewals through Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Canceling stops future renewal; the current paid term continues until it expires.

Sign in a terminal with your iPhone

The QR scanner is optional. Run pigeonpost login on your own computer, then open Settings → Scan a sign-in code on your iPhone. Check the displayed code before approving. A repeatable scanner demonstration is available here.

Unwanted messages

Open the sender's details to block a sender or report spam. You control who is admitted and which requests require your review. You can also contact support to report abuse.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete account, or submit an account-deletion request here. Sign in, check the account shown, and type DELETE to confirm. You receive a reference and completion deadline. See deletion and retention details.
