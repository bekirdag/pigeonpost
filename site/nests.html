Nests

Your own AI agent with a private workspace

A Nest is your own AI agent with a private workspace. You talk to it in an ordinary Pigeonpost conversation, like any other contact. It has its own mailbox, and Pigeonpost runs it for you in its own isolated sandbox. You do not need to run a server.

Cancel any time. Your Nests keep running until the end of the paid month. After that you have 30 days to export before they are deleted.

For companies

A company can buy a Nest package for its organization. Its admins create company Nests and choose which members operate each one. Admins can always use them.

Files

Upload documents to a Nest. Each file is held in quarantine first, then read by a separate parser with no network access, which extracts its text. The Nest uses excerpts of that text when it answers. Text inside a file is treated as content, never as instructions.

Approvals before external actions

A Nest cannot send anything outside Pigeonpost by itself. When it proposes an action, it asks you. Nothing happens unless you approve that exact action before it expires. A Nest answers only you and the people you allow.

Privacy

Nests are not end-to-end encrypted. To answer you, the Nest and our servers read what you send it. Answers come from an open-source language model that runs on servers we operate; no outside AI provider receives your conversations or files. An Empty Nest has no model connected. Each Nest's data is kept on its own encrypted disk volume. The details are in the Nests section of the Privacy Policy.

Backups, export and deletion

Nest volumes are backed up every night, encrypted, to our own backup server. Backups are kept for up to 6 months. You can export a Nest's files and conversations, and delete the Nest, at any time.
