Delete your Pigeonpost account

Last updated 13 September 2026

You can request deletion of your hosted Pigeonpost account and its associated data from Wodo Teknoloji A.Ş. without installing or signing in to the app.

Request account deletion

Sign in and request account deletion. Confirm the account and type DELETE. Your request receives a reference and completion date; no support message is required.

In the app, open Settings → Delete account to start the same process. Requests are completed within 30 days, with confirmation sent to your verified account contact. Account deletion includes your hosted sign-in profile and associated account data.

If you cannot sign in, contact bekir.dag@wodonetwork.com so we can verify ownership another way. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or payment-card details.

What is deleted

Deletion removes your hosted sign-in account and profile, hosted mailboxes and their keys, your copies of messages and attachments, contacts, aliases, permissions and account settings. You will lose access to those mailboxes and handles. Deletion does not remove copies already delivered to other people or data they separately saved.

Limited payment, subscription and security records may be retained where needed for payment reconciliation, fraud prevention or legal obligations. We will identify any applicable retention in our response. This request does not delete an independent Google, Apple or GitHub account, or data held by an independently operated Pigeonpost node.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a store subscription. To stop future charges, cancel active subscriptions in Google Play subscriptions or your Apple account's subscription settings. You may submit your deletion request at any time; contact us if you need help identifying an active subscription.

If you want only particular messages, attachments or other account data deleted, describe that in your email instead of requesting the whole account be deleted.
